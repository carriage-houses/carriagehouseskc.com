News
  • With the fall weather upon us, we have scheduled a "Volunteer Work Day" on Saturday, November 7. We'll start around 8:30 AM with coffee and breakfast — bring work gloves, a broom, a shovel, a rake, etc. We'll also be grilling burgers and brats for lunch, so bring a side dish to share. More details to follow.
  • We have a new supply of visor remotes for the front gate for $30 each. Contact Jim Dean in G5 at board@chgoa.com if you would like to purchase one.

You will be able to control your data in the owner’s directory, access the Rules and Regulations, notify of a maintenance issue, and request remotes and other items. And don’t forget we have a private Facebook group for owners.
