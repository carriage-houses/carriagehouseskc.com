Welcome to the Carriage Houses of Johnson County Newsletter. Here you will find News, Owner of the Month, Events, and the Carriage Houses Marketplace.

News

Owners Only Website — Thanks to the hard work by Joe Francis, the Owners Only Website is up and running. Visit members.carriagehouseskc.com. Simply enter your email address and you will get an email with a link to the website — no password required. Here you will be able to control who can see your information in the owners’ directory, use the interactive facility map, get a copy of the Rules and Regulations, report a maintenance issue, or request things like visor gate remotes, etc. And don’t forget we have a private Facebook group, “Carriage Houses of Johnson County Owners Group”.

Workday — Our first owner workday was a big success! More than 30 owners turned out, and by the end of the day we had finished the whole list and then some: dead trees removed, the front gate and fence repaired, the entrance pruned and cleaned up, drainage and erosion control along the creek bank, and a long list of fixes around the buildings. The weathered barn door that everyone saw from the main entrance is finally gone, and many owners told us how glad they were to see it go. Thanks to John Pauley for loaning his excavator and skid steer, which made the heavy work possible, and to Eric Offill for the Carriage House T-shirts. Coffee and doughnuts got us started, burgers and brats kept us going, and best of all, owners met each other and made new friends. Another workday is planned for spring, so watch for the date and join us.

Owner of the Month

Andy Poecker — You will likely recognize Andy from Heritage Electric doing the electrical work on new buildings and remodel work on existing buildings. But Andy is a long-time garage owner, car guy, and routinely volunteers for all types of work needed on the property. Next time you see Andy, ask him why he is often called the “Mayor of Milwaukee”.
