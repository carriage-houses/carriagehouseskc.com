Owner Information

Owners Website

The Owners Only Website is up and running. Visit members.carriagehouseskc.com — simply enter your email address and you'll get an email with a link to the site. No password required.

Once signed in, you can:

  • Control who can see your information in the owners’ directory
  • Use the interactive facility map
  • Get a copy of the Rules and Regulations
  • Report a maintenance issue
  • Request visor gate remotes and other items

Sign in to the Owners Website →

Owners Facebook Group

Stay connected with your fellow owners through our private Facebook group. This is an owners-only community where you can ask questions, share what's happening at the facility, coordinate events, and keep up with news from other members of Carriage Houses of Johnson County.

Access is restricted to current owners — you'll need to request to join and your ownership will be verified before you're approved.

Join the Owners Facebook Group →
