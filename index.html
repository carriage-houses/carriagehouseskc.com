Carriage Houses Country Club

A hobby garage condo community

29230 W 83rd Street • DeSoto, KS 66018

Own Your Space

Carriage Houses offers privately owned garage condominiums — not rentals — for car enthusiasts, hobbyists, and collectors in the Kansas City area. Whether you need space for classic cars, hot rods, motorcycles, boats, ATVs, RVs, motorhomes, or a dedicated workshop, this community was built for you.

Located on the east side of DeSoto, Kansas, our facility is a beautiful campus developed by four retired Air Force veteran fighter pilots with a vision to create a unique and fun community of people who wanted a place of their own.
