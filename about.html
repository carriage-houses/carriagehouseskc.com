Carriage Houses

Carriage Houses of Johnson County is a beautiful garage condominium facility located on the east side of DeSoto, Kansas. Developed by four retired Air Force veteran fighter pilots who had a vision to create a unique and fun community of people that wanted a place of their own to enjoy and use for various hobbies, work spaces, vehicles, and activities.

Work began in 2008. The first building of 22 garages opened, and new owners took possession in August of 2011. Today the facility spans a large campus with 15 buildings housing 267 garages across approximately 19 acres.

Community & Events

The facility features recreational amenities including an American Bandstand-style stage, a pickleball court, a basketball goal, and beautifully landscaped areas for gathering.
